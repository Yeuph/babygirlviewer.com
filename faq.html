Common questions

Where can I download Babygirl?

Not yet. A small group of testers gets an early alpha first; a public beta is planned for December.

Does it run on a Mac or Linux?

No. Babygirl runs on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with an NVIDIA or AMD graphics card.

Does it have voice chat? Can I log in with a multi-factor code?

Not yet. If your Second Life account uses multi-factor authentication, Babygirl can't log in to it yet.

I need help with my Second Life account: password, billing, Linden dollars.

We can't help with that. Contact Linden Lab through Second Life support.

Is Babygirl an approved Second Life viewer?

Not yet: it isn't on Linden Lab's Third Party Viewer Directory. Babygirl is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.

Found a bug? See Support.

Babygirl is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.
