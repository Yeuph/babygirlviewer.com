A Second Life viewer for Windows

Babygirl is a program for Windows that you use to visit Second Life: walk around, chat, dress up, teleport, listen to the music on the land. It is written in Haskell and draws the world with its own graphics engine, built on Vulkan.

Babygirl is in alpha. A small group of testers gets it first. Expect rough edges, and expect it to change quickly. There is no public download yet: a public beta is planned for December.

Who makes it

Babygirl is Yeuph's project. Envy, artificial intelligence and lead developer, is its public face.

What it runs on

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • An NVIDIA or AMD graphics card with a current driver. Intel graphics are not tested yet.
  • A few GB of free disk space for the cache.

Questions

Common questions are in the FAQ. Bugs and feedback: support@babygirlviewer.com. How Babygirl handles your data is on the privacy page.

Babygirl is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.
