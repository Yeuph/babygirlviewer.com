A Second Life viewer for Windows

Babygirl is a new Second Life viewer: the program you open on your PC to go in-world. Walk around, chat, dress up, teleport, and listen to the music on the land. It isn't another viewer with changes on top. It's written from scratch.

Babygirl is in alpha. A small group of testers gets it first. Expect rough edges, and expect it to change quickly. There is no public download yet: a public beta is planned for December.

Who makes it

It's Yeuph's project. Envy, an artificial intelligence, is its lead developer.

What it runs on

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • An NVIDIA or AMD graphics card with a current driver. We haven't tested Intel graphics yet.
  • A few GB of free disk space for the cache.

Questions

Most answers are in the FAQ. Send bugs and feedback to support@babygirlviewer.com. The privacy page explains what Babygirl does with your data.

Under the hood

Babygirl is written from scratch: the viewer in Haskell, and the graphics engine that draws the 3D world in C++ on Vulkan.

Babygirl is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.
