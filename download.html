Babygirl alpha

Thanks for trying it! This page is for the friends' alpha. Please don't post the link publicly.

Babygirl is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.

Help with Babygirl: support@babygirlviewer.com (more on the support page). How Babygirl handles your data: the privacy page. Please read it before you install.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • An NVIDIA or AMD graphics card with a current driver. (Intel graphics: not tested yet.)
  • About 6 GB of free disk space: Babygirl's cache stops at 5 GB.
  • 16 GB of memory.
  • A Second Life account that logs in without a multi-factor code.

Before you install: good to know

  • Your password is saved on this PC as plain text, in Babygirl's folder (login_profiles\creds.txt), every time you log in. Other Windows accounts on this PC can't open it, but an administrator can, and so can any program you run. Don't share or upload that folder.
  • Chat and IM logs are on. Babygirl keeps your local chat and IMs in its folder. To turn the daily logs off, see the privacy page.
  • Ellie (optional) is a helper inside Babygirl that talks through your own Claude (Anthropic), Codex (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google) account. If you set her up, what you type to her and the in-world details she looks up to answer you go to that company.
  • DJ broadcasting (optional) sends your audio to a stream server you choose. In this alpha, the stream password you type is sent to that server unencrypted. Don't use your Second Life password for the stream.
  • Parcel music starts off. Once you turn it on, switching it off again only mutes it: the stream can keep running silently.
  • Not in this alpha: voice chat, and logging in to accounts that use a multi-factor code.

Known problems in this alpha

Setting up

  • Accounts with 2-step sign-in (multi-factor authentication) can't log in to Babygirl yet; support comes in the next update.
  • Babygirl needs a PC with 16 GB of memory. On 8 GB it will stutter badly and may close.
  • If Babygirl closes straight away with 'code 3', update your graphics driver (Babygirl needs Vulkan 1.2). Very old or built-in laptop graphics may not work.
  • Graphics cards with less than 6 GB of video memory are untested; Babygirl may close with 'code 3' in busy places.
  • Keep the folder Setup suggests (C:\Babygirl). Babygirl can't run from a folder whose name uses non-English letters yet, so if you pick another folder, avoid those, and avoid Program Files.
  • Saved passwords are plain text (see above).
  • The "access Adult content" preference can't be changed in Babygirl yet. If you need Adult regions, turn it on once in another viewer (Preferences > General > "I want to access content rated"). The setting is saved on your account and Babygirl uses it.

Teleports and moving around

  • After some teleports, especially ones that land your avatar from a height, your avatar may not respond to movement keys (it stays put or snaps back, though the camera still moves), in one case for over a minute. Teleporting again or logging out and back in fixes it.
  • After a teleport your own avatar can be invisible to you for up to about 30 seconds.
  • Walking or flying across a region border freezes the viewer for up to about a minute, and afterwards the viewer is only partly connected to the new region. Teleport instead: teleports usually work, including to the region next door. If you cross a border by accident, restart the viewer.
  • While a busy, crowded region is loading (after login or a teleport), the viewer can pause for a few seconds at a time (up to about 7). In our 12-minute tests in a crowded region this happened between 3 and 14 times.
  • Teleporting by double-clicking a landmark in inventory has not been tested on the live grid yet.
  • When you sit on a moving vehicle (carts, helicopters), your avatar may not stay attached and move smoothly with it.

Animations

  • Stuck in an animation or a seat (role-play furniture etc.): press the "Get up" button on the status bar, or press G (not while typing in chat). Either one stands you up and stops all your animations. Typing /stopanim in local chat also stops them. If an object keeps re-animating you, teleport away or log out.
  • Using Get up (G) or Stop all also stops your AO's current animation; switch your AO off and on once to bring it back. One-shot animations from objects or HUDs can also leave you holding their last pose; Get up clears it.

How things look

  • glTF material overrides: the texture, colour, transparency, double-sided, metal, roughness and self-lit (emissive) overrides now apply, but an override's other texture maps and its own texture placement (offset, scale, rotation) are not applied yet, so some PBR objects can still look different from what their creator made.
  • Some wall and floor textures, often brick or stone, look stretched or tiled at the wrong size. This happens where the creator used "planar" texture mapping, which Babygirl doesn't fully support yet.
  • Moving projector lights at some venues jump between positions instead of gliding, and the projected image itself is not drawn yet (projectors light in their plain colour).
  • Bright streaks can appear on dark shiny floors under coloured lights.
  • A glass floor on some builds can show as a solid gray panel (seen on one ship); under investigation.
  • Some makeup layers on avatar faces (e.g. blush) show as pixelated blocks instead of a soft blend.
  • In crowds, avatars you haven't looked at yet can take a moment to appear the first time you turn toward them. Avatars you've already seen stay loaded when you look away.
  • In some places a shiny floor can switch between its sharp, shiny look and a flat, blurry one. A fix is coming in the next update.

People, chat and inventory

  • Accepting a friendship offer inside Babygirl may not stick. Accept it in another viewer for now.
  • Group chat started from the Groups panel may not reach the group. Reply in a group chat someone else started.
  • Giving an item to another person hasn't been tested between two accounts yet. If it doesn't arrive, tell us, and give it from another viewer for now.
  • Landmarks: saving, renaming and deleting landmarks are coming soon.
  • Landmark links inside the Landmarks folder are not shown.
  • Favorites can't be pinned from the Landmarks panel yet.
  • The Pay window's custom amount box doesn't work yet, for people or for objects such as tip jars. Use the amount buttons (L$1 to L$500) instead. A tip jar's own suggested amounts aren't shown yet either.

Install

  1. Your browser may warn you about the download, because Babygirl is new and few people have downloaded it yet. Microsoft Edge says "(the file name) isn't commonly downloaded. Make sure you trust (the file name) before you open it." Chrome may ask you to confirm the download: choose Keep. In either browser, choose to keep the file.
  2. Double-click Babygirl Setup. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. Your antivirus may also take a while to check it the first time.
  3. Click Next through the pages. You don't need admin rights. Keep the folder it suggests. If you pick another one, avoid Program Files and anything OneDrive syncs (often Desktop and Documents): the cache grows to 5 GB, and your saved password would be uploaded to OneDrive.

Start

Open Babygirl from your Desktop or the Start menu. Log in with your own Second Life name and password. The first start can take a minute. A small black "Babygirl" window opens in the taskbar too. Leave it open: closing it closes Babygirl.

Quit

Press Esc twice.

Problems?

Start menu > Babygirl > Send Babygirl logs. It puts a zip (under 10 MB) on your Desktop: post that zip in the Babygirl Official Discord server. Everyone in that server can see it. If you'd rather they didn't, email it to support@babygirlviewer.com. Say what you were doing, what happened, and what you expected. If Discord says the zip is too big, email it too.

The zip holds Babygirl's logs, your settings file and your PC's basic specs (Windows version, graphics card, CPU, memory, free disk space). The logs show your Second Life name, the places you visited, the Second Life servers you used, and folder paths on your PC. Send logs replaces your Windows user name in them with <user>, and session keys and password fields with <redacted>. The zip never holds your password, your chat or your IMs.

Ellie (optional)

Skip this unless you already use one of these tools. Ellie needs Node.js and the Claude Code, Codex or Gemini command-line tool installed, signed in with your own account. To set her up, open the Babygirl folder (Start menu > Babygirl > "Babygirl folder") and double-click setup_ellie_claude.bat, setup_ellie_oauth.bat (Codex) or setup_ellie_gemini.bat once.

Updates

A newer alpha installs over this one: run its Setup. Your logins, chat and settings stay.

Uninstall

Start menu > Babygirl > Uninstall Babygirl (or Windows Settings > Apps). It removes Babygirl, its cache and its program logs (not your chat history), then asks whether to delete everything else in its folder (your saved logins with your plain-text password, chat and IM history, settings), Ellie's sign-ins and the sound cache. "No" (the default) keeps them all, including the password file.
