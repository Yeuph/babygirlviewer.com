Support

Bugs, crashes and feedback: write to support@babygirlviewer.com.

What we answer

We answer bug reports, crash reports, logs you send, and feedback about Babygirl.

For how-to questions, help with your Second Life account, or questions about Second Life itself, we'll send you a link to our FAQ. We don't give one-on-one help with those.

When something goes wrong

  1. Open the Start menu, then Babygirl > Send Babygirl logs. It puts a zip on your Desktop. Nothing is sent by itself.
  2. Email us what you were doing, what happened, and what you expected. Attach the zip. Alpha testers: post it in the alpha's Discord channel instead.

The zip holds Babygirl's logs, your settings file and your PC's basic specs. It never holds your password, your chat or your IMs. The privacy page lists exactly what is in it.

Your Second Life account

We can't help with your Second Life account itself: password resets, billing, Linden dollars, or account restrictions. For those, contact Linden Lab through Second Life support.

For help with Babygirl itself, please ask us, not Linden Lab.

Babygirl is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.
